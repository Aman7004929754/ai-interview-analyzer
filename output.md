<p><strong>Minor Project Report Structure</strong></p><p><strong>(for BTech Programs)</strong></p><p>Preliminary Pages</p><p>●	Cover Page: Standardized institutional format.</p><p>●	Certificate: Signed by the Supervisor and the HOD/ASSOCIATE HOD</p><p>●	Declaration of Originality by the Student </p><p>●	Acknowledgement</p><p>●	Abstract</p><p>(A one-page summary highlighting the Problem, Methodology, Key Results, and Societal Relevance)</p><p>●	Table of Contents </p><p>●	List of Figures (Captions with Numbering)</p><p>●	List of Tables (Titles with Numbering)</p><p>●	List of Abbreviations</p><p><strong>Chapter 1: Introduction</strong></p><ul><li><ol><li>Background</li></ol></li></ul><p>1.2	Problem Statement</p><p>1.3	Social &amp; Environmental Relevance</p><p>1.4	Gantt Chart / Timeline </p><p>1.5 	Scope  </p><p><strong>Chapter 2: Literature Survey/Critical Review</strong>   </p><p>2.1	Overview of Existing Work/ Literature Review</p><p>2.2	Summary Table</p><p>2.3	Identification of research gaps/ Limitations of Existing Methods</p><p><strong>Chapter 3: System Design &amp; Methodology</strong></p><p>3.1	Proposed Methodology (use block diagrams/ flowcharts)</p><p>3.2	Tool and Technique Selection (why you choose a particular tool/technique)</p><p><strong>Chapter 4: Implementation &amp; Testing</strong></p><p>4.1	Experimental Setup </p><p>4.2	Test Cases/ Case study/Code / flow chart/Screenshots / output<br /></p><p><strong>Chapter 5: Results &amp; Discussion</strong></p><p>5.1	Result analysis </p><p>5.2	Performance Evaluation/Comparison with existing methods</p><p><strong>Chapter 6: Conclusion &amp; Future Scope</strong></p><p>6.1	Summary of Work and Achievements (award, paper publication etc., if any)</p><p>6.2	Impact on society, Environmental sustainability Ethical issues and compliance (Industry relevance, if any)</p><p>6.3	Limitations of the Work</p><p>6.4	Future Scope </p><p>	</p><p><strong>References &amp; Appendices</strong></p><p>●	References: Use a standard format (IEEE). Ensure that at least 50% are from recent (last 5 years) journal</p><p>●	Appendices: Datasheets, long code snippets, or raw survey data.</p><p>●	Annexure: </p><ul><li>Outcome of the report: Proof of (Application/ product/ Journal Research paper/Conference research paper/ Accepted Abstract(conference)/ patent) </li><li>Sustainability Statement: A dedicated section on how the project affects the environment/ society.</li><li>Team Roles: Defining what Student A, B, and C specifically contributed.</li></ul><p><strong>Formatting Specifications</strong></p><p>Feature	Requirement</p><p>Paper Size		A4, White</p><p>Font Style		Times New Roman</p><p>Font Size		12 pt (Body), 14 pt (Sub-headings), 16 pt (Chapter Titles)</p><p>Line Spacing		1.5 </p><p>Margins		2 cm on all sides</p><p>Page Numbering	Bottom-centre (Roman for prelims, Arabic for chapters)</p><p>Binding			spiral</p><p>Final Review Checklist (Before Submission)</p><ul><li>Ensure there are no spelling or grammatical errors. </li><li>Maintain consistent formatting throughout the document. </li><li>Verify that all figures and tables are properly numbered, titled/ captioned, and referred within the text. </li><li>Ensure that figure captions are placed <strong>below</strong> the figures. </li><li>Ensure that table titles are placed <strong>above</strong> the tables. </li><li>Confirm that all equations are correctly numbered and appropriately referred in the text. </li><li>Check that no section is left empty or incomplete.</li></ul>
